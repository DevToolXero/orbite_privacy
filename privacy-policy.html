Privacy Policy — Orbite

Last updated: August 2026

Orbite does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions.

Accounts and advertising

Orbite has no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools of any kind.

Offline operation

The app runs entirely offline and does not connect to the internet.

Data storage

Game progress is saved only on your device. It is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.
